If we treat NovelVerse as a completely free novel-reading and publishing platform, the architecture becomes much cleaner.
There should be no premium chapters, paywalls, subscription checks, or paid audio in the core reading experience. Instead, NovelVerse can grow through community, authors, discovery, reading streaks, friendships, groups, comments, and engagement.
Below is the redesigned full Reading + Social ecosystem, including the actual user flows and interactions.

The Core Principle for NovelVerse
I would design NovelVerse around three loops:
📖 Reading Loop
Discover
 ↓
Read
 ↓
React
 ↓
Finish
 ↓
Next Chapter
 ↓
Next Novel
👥 Social Loop
Read
 ↓
React
 ↓
Comment
 ↓
Meet Reader
 ↓
Friend
 ↓
Chat
 ↓
Reading Room
 ↓
Read Together
🔥 Engagement Loop
Read
 ↓
Progress
 ↓
Streak
 ↓
Achievement
 ↓
Challenge
 ↓
Leaderboard
 ↓
Read More
When these three loops work together, NovelVerse stops feeling like "a website where I read books" and starts feeling like a social reading community.
And because you're making it completely free, the focus should be on great reading UX + discovery + community + author-reader relationships, rather than designing the system around monetization.
